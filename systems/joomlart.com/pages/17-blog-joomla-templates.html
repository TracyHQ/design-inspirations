Introducing JA Voyara: An Elegant Travel Agency Joomla Template

The journeys you plan are unforgettable - your website should be too. Meet JA Voyara, our elegant new Joomla template made for travel agencies, tour operators and travel planners.

JA Voyara brings your destinations, curated journeys and travel stories together in a warm, magazine-style design that feels premium the moment a visitor arrives. Immersive homepage layouts put your featured trips, destination highlights and traveller reviews front and center. Card-based destination listings surface location, duration, travel style, ratings and pricing at a glance, while spacious editorial travel stories - complete with full-width photography and author details - turn inspiration into bookings. A clear, flexible pricing page presents your tour packages, planning services or memberships with feature comparisons, FAQs and testimonials, and beautifully designed light and dark modes keep it all looking refined, day or night. Built on the T4 Framework and running on Joomla 6, it's responsive, fast, and ready to customize - no coding required.

  • Best for Travel & Tourism
  • T4 Framework
  • Joomla 6
  • Light & Dark modes

A warm, editorial style that sells the journey

JA Voyara brings destinations, curated journeys and authentic travel stories together in a refined, magazine-style design. It's built to make a memorable first impression - and to turn browsers into bookings - without you needing to write a single line of code.

Everything a travel website needs

Beautiful destination & journey listings

Present your tours through elegant card layouts that highlight location, duration, travel style, ratings, pricing and featured trips - so visitors find the right experience at a glance.

Travel stories & editorial content

Turn inspiration into bookings with a dedicated experiences section and spacious travel-story layouts - full-width photography, author info, topic tags and related articles that keep readers engaged.

A clear, flexible pricing page

Show your packages, planning services or memberships with attractive pricing cards, feature comparisons, FAQs and testimonials - making it easy for travellers to choose and book.

Beautiful light & dark modes

A warm palette, refined typography and balanced contrast keep your premium travel look consistent in both light and dark styles - comfortable to browse in any environment.

JA Voyara is built on the T4 Framework and runs on Joomla 6 - responsive, fast, and simple to customize with a visual layout builder. All standard Joomla pages come fully styled, too.

Who it's for

  • Travel agencies & tour operators that want a website as polished as their trips
  • Independent travel planners building trust with a professional online presence
  • Experience-led tourism brands that tell stories and sell curated journeys

The pages that close deals

  • Pricing - present plans and compare features clearly
