Frequently Asked Questions

Which Joomla versions are supported?

All our products fully support Joomla 4 and Joomla 5. Our team is actively integrating Joomla 6 to be ready upon its release.

What's on the roadmap for 2026?

We're investing heavily in the future. In 2026, our members will receive:

🚀 Core Platform

  • Joomla 6 support as soon as it's released — JoomlArt is always first to integrate the latest versions
  • Dark/Light mode across all templates — meeting modern UX standards

🎨 Expanded Template Library

  • 15+ new Joomla templates for diverse use cases: corporate, e-commerce, education, blog, portfolio, agency, and more
  • T4 Page Builder Premium with 30+ additional content blocks

🤖 AI-Powered Features

  • AI Analytics: smart analysis of your own website — it reviews your content, structure and SEO, checks how well your site is optimized for AI and search engines to truly understand it, and pinpoints exactly what to improve.
  • AI Customer Service: faster, smarter support for JoomlArt customers — an AI assistant available 24/7 to answer your questions and help resolve issues the moment they arise, so you're never left waiting.

Investing in JoomlArt today means owning a Joomla site that stays at the forefront of web technology — never outdated.

Which plan should I choose?

It depends on your specific needs:

  • I have a single, simple Joomla site → JA Starter ($99/year)
  • I'm a freelancer with multiple Joomla sites needing diverse templates → JA Template ($149/year)
  • I need to build a community or social network on my site → JomSocial Pro ($149/6m)
  • I'm an agency or developer needing everything → JoomlArt One ($299/year)
  • I already have a template and just need plugins → JA Extensions ($59/year)

See the detailed comparison table above for a complete breakdown.

What's the difference between T4 Page Builder Pro and Premium?
  • T4 Pro (~30 basic blocks): included in JA Template and JoomlArt One
  • T4 Premium (120+ blocks, advanced features, client content update permission): exclusive to JoomlArt One

JA Template members who want T4 Premium can upgrade to JoomlArt One anytime.

How does auto-renew work?

Your subscription automatically renews at the end of each billing cycle so you never lose access to updates and support. You can cancel anytime from your member area — and you'll continue using your plan until the end of the paid period.

A reminder email is sent 14 days before each auto-renewal.

How do renewals work?

All plans allow you to use downloaded templates and extensions permanently during your active subscription. Renewal is required if you wish to:

  • Continue receiving technical support
  • Download new releases and updates
  • Access newly released templates and extensions

Renewals are a simple 1-click process from your member area.

Can I upgrade to a higher plan?

Yes, anytime. Available upgrade paths:

  • JA Starter → JA Template
  • JA Template → JoomlArt One
  • JomSocial Pro → JoomlArt One

Open a ticket in your member area, and our admin will process the upgrade within 24 hours.

What's your refund policy?

7 days, no questions asked. If a product isn't right for you, open a ticket within 7 days of purchase and receive a 100% refund. See full details in our Refund Policy.
