Frequently asked questions

What is GEO / AI visibility?
Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is about making your site easy for AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity to find, understand, trust, and cite — the way SEO optimizes for traditional search.
Which JoomlArt plans include GeoReport?
GeoReport is included with three plans: JA Starter, JA Template and JoomlArt One. It is not part of the JA Extensions or JomSocial Pro plans. You can compare every plan on our pricing page.
Do I need to pay extra for GeoReport?
No extra purchase — GeoReport is included if you're on the JA Starter, JA Template or JoomlArt One plan. Usage quotas are set by GeoReport based on your plan.
Do I need a new GeoReport account?
No — please don't create a separate account. On an eligible plan, just sign in with your JoomlArt account and your access is applied automatically.
How do I activate it?
Head to GeoReport and sign in with your JoomlArt account. That's it.
What's available today?
Site Audit and the Chrome extension are live now. Content Audit, GEO Landing Builder and GEO Content Writer are coming soon.
