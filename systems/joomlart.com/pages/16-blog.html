Dark Mode reached 10 more templates

The roll-out that started in June carried straight through July, across two waves. Each of these templates now includes a one-click sun / moon toggle, automatic detection of the visitor's system preference, and a remembered choice for returning visitors - while your existing light design stays exactly the same.

Templates updated with Dark Mode in July

  • JA Teline V - our best-selling news & magazine template
  • JA Athena - university & education
  • JA MixStore - multipurpose eCommerce (VirtueMart & J2Store)
  • JA Phio - portfolio & creative showcase
  • JA Helple - charity, NGO & fundraising
  • JA FindUs - business & directory
  • JA Vital - medical, hospital & healthcare
  • JA Sport - sports teams & football / soccer clubs
  • JA Coach - coaching, online courses & LMS
  • JA Resort - hotel & resort booking (Solidres)

That takes the running total to 23 templates with built-in Dark Mode since the roll-out began. Switching it on is a single template update - your content and settings stay exactly where they are. Prefer light only? Leave it: light mode remains the default and untouched.

Joomla MCP: manage your site by talking to an AI

The biggest new thing in July wasn't a template. Joomla MCP connects your Joomla site to an AI assistant - Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini - so you can handle everyday website work in plain language instead of clicking through administrator screens.

Ask it to publish an article, tidy up categories, find a piece of content or check what changed, and it does the work against your real site.

How it works

  • Nothing is installed on your website - it uses Joomla's own built-in web services
  • You stay in control - permissions follow your existing Joomla user, and you can revoke access at any time
  • Set-up takes about five minutes - enable the built-in features, create a personal access key, connect your assistant
  • Works with Joomla 4, 5 and 6

Try Joomla MCP →

In case you missed it: GeoReport

It landed just days before our June recap went out, so it deserves a second mention. GeoReport measures your website's AI visibility - whether ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity can find, trust and cite your site - and gives you the exact fixes.

It crawls your site and rolls everything into a single GEO Health Score built from four benchmarks: Search Alignment, Layout, Readability and Credibility. You get severity-ranked issues with step-by-step fixes, a side-by-side comparison across the four AI models, and a Chrome extension to audit any page - even a competitor's.

7 Joomla Templates Get Dark Mode in One Week

Dark Mode is moving quickly across the JoomlArt template collection. In just one week, seven Joomla templates have been updated with built-in light and dark mode support.

This is part of our ongoing effort to keep JoomlArt templates modern, flexible, and easier to use for real websites. Whether you run a business site, magazine, travel portal, non-profit project, or agriculture brand, your visitors can now enjoy a cleaner browsing experience in both light and dark styles.

No redesign required. These updates are built into the templates, so your existing light design remains untouched while Dark Mode becomes available as an additional viewing option.

7 Templates Updated with Dark Mode

This week’s rollout brings Dark Mode support to the following templates:

JA Vega

Business and corporate Joomla template with a modern professional design.

JA Morgan

Multipurpose business template for companies, agencies, and service websites.

JA Impact

NGO and non-profit template for campaigns, events, donations, and causes.

JA Vogue

Fashion, lifestyle, and magazine template with strong visual storytelling.

JA Trip

Travel and tour template for destinations, tour packages, and travel blogs.

JA Spark

Modern multipurpose Joomla template for creative and business websites.

JA Agrivita

Agriculture and organic farming template for farms, food brands, and green businesses.

What Dark Mode Adds to Your Website

Dark Mode gives your visitors more control over how they view your site. Some users prefer a brighter interface during the day, while others prefer a darker layout at night or in low-light environments.

With these updates, each supported template now offers a more flexible front-end experience without changing your content structure, menu setup, modules, or existing layouts.

Main highlights

  • Built-in Light / Dark Mode support
  • Clean sun / moon toggle for visitors
  • Automatic system preference detection
  • Remembered mode selection for returning visitors
  • Dark styling refined across key template pages
  • Existing Light Mode design preserved
  • Simple template update process

Why We Are Rolling This Out Across More Templates

Dark Mode is no longer just a visual trend. It has become a common expectation across apps, operating systems, dashboards, and modern websites.

For Joomla site owners, this means a site can feel more current and user-friendly without needing a complete redesign. That is why we are gradually bringing Dark Mode to more templates in the JoomlArt library, starting with popular templates across different categories.

The goal is simple: help your existing Joomla website stay fresh, while giving you more design flexibility for new projects.

A Better Experience Across Different Website Types

Each template serves a different type of website, so the Dark Mode treatment has been adjusted to fit the purpose of each design.

For business websites

Templates like JA Vega, JA Morgan, and JA Spark benefit from a sharper, more polished presentation that works well for corporate profiles, service pages, portfolios, and landing pages.

For content-heavy websites

JA Vogue and JA Trip can now offer a more comfortable reading experience for magazine articles, travel stories, destination guides, and blog content.

For cause-driven and niche websites

JA Impact and JA Agrivita now support a modern dark interface while keeping the warmth and clarity needed for non-profit, agriculture, organic, and community-focused websites.

How to Update Your Template

If you are already using one of the updated templates, you can access Dark Mode by installing the latest template package from JoomlArt.

  1. Go to your JoomlArt account and download the latest version of your template.
  2. Install the update over your current template version.
  3. Open your site and test the Light / Dark Mode toggle in the front-end header.
  4. Review your main pages to make sure everything looks right with your own content and images.

Your existing content, modules, and template settings remain in place during the update.

If your website uses heavy custom CSS or third-party extensions, we recommend checking the main pages after updating to make sure custom sections also look good in Dark Mode.

More Updates Are Coming

This seven-template rollout is another step in our wider update plan for the JoomlArt collection. We will continue improving templates with modern features, compatibility updates, design refinements, and Joomla platform support.

If your favorite template is not in this batch yet, stay tuned. More Dark Mode updates and template improvements are on the way.
