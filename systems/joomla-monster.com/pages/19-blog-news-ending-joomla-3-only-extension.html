Ending Joomla 3-Only Extensions and Templates

As Joomla continues advancing with modern, faster, and more secure releases like Joomla 4 and Joomla 5, we believe it’s the right moment to part ways with products built exclusively for Joomla 3.

To dedicate our efforts to creating high-quality tools fully optimized for the latest Joomla ecosystem, we’ve decided to officially retire the following Joomla 3-only extensions:

  • DJ-Reviews
  • DJ-Flyer
  • DJ-LikeBox
  • DJ-EmbedLight
  • DJ-PassReset
  • DJ-Chat
  • DJ-Suggester
  • DJ-CookieMonster
  • DJ-Cannonical
  • DJ-Messages

In addition, we are discontinuing all legacy Joomla 3 templates previously provided by Joomla-Monster. Since these templates were designed for Joomla 3 and have not been updated for Joomla 4 or 5, they are either no longer available for download or will be removed shortly.

Check Out Our Joomla 4 & 5 Extensions

Why Are We Making This Change?

Joomla 3 officially reached its end of life in August 2023. Continuing to support outdated versions limits our ability to invest in creating and enhancing tools for the future of Joomla. For this reason, we are shifting our development focus toward modern, reliable extensions and templates designed specifically for Joomla 4 and Joomla 5.

By doing so, we can:

  • Deliver stronger security and performance
  • Provide improved support and more frequent updates
  • Build tools that fully leverage Joomla’s latest innovations

What This Means for You

If you are still running Joomla 3 or using any of the extensions listed above, we highly recommend uninstalling them from your site. Since these products are no longer supported and are incompatible with Joomla 4 and Joomla 5, keeping them may expose your website to technical problems or security risks.

We encourage you to transition to our actively maintained extensions and templates, which are fully optimized for today’s Joomla versions.

Looking Ahead Together

Although it’s never easy to part with long-standing products, this transition marks a key step toward building a more streamlined and future-ready Joomla experience for everyone.

We’re enthusiastic about what lies ahead and invite you to discover our expanding collection of responsive, feature-rich extensions and templates, fully optimized for Joomla 4 and Joomla 5.

Need assistance with migration or selecting the best tools for your updated site? Our team is ready to support you every step of the way.
