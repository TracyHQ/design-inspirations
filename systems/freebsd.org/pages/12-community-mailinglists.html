Mailing Lists

Mailing lists are the primary communication channel for the FreeBSD community, and cover many topic areas:

  • https://lists.freebsd.org/

  • frequently asked questions.

Archives

The threaded archives with all e-mails since 1994 are available at https://mail-archive.freebsd.org/mail/

Browse:

  • https://lists.freebsd.org/archives/

Search:

  • https://lists.freebsd.org/search

Archives prior to May 2021 are in a separate area.
