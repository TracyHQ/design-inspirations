Why use Grafana Cloud for cost management and optimization?

See exactly where your observability budget goes

  • Track spending in real time with a centralized app that shows spend balance, contract burn-down, and product-level usage

  • Spot anomalies early by identifying unusual cost patterns and comparing month-over-month changes to optimize your spend, and identify trends before they impact your budget

  • Access detailed breakdowns with monthly invoices you view and download directly in Grafana Cloud

Stop budget overruns before they happen

  • Avoid unexpected charges with configurable alerts that set thresholds on specific usage metrics, detect spikes or anomalies in cost or usage, and surface attribution insights showing which teams or services are driving changes

  • Get early warnings by notifying teams at pre-set percentages of budget spend, or when attribution or anomaly-based signals suggest unusual spend patterns, so they can take action before limits are reached

  • Route alerts anywhere by sending notifications to Slack, email, PagerDuty, or any channel your team uses

Reduce costs and maximize insight automatically with Adaptive Telemetry

  • Cut costs by 30-50% on average with Adaptive Telemetry, which automatically identifies and optimizes low-value data across all telemetry 

  • Accelerate resolution by making sure the right signals are surfaced quickly

  • Highlight the data that matters by using exemptions to preserve specific signals critical to your operations

Attribute costs to teams, projects, and services

  • Allocate every dollar clearly by breaking down costs by team, project, service, or any custom dimension using cost attribution

  • Build team accountability by giving every dollar an owner so teams understand their observability impact

  • Support internal billing by enabling chargeback and showback models without forcing teams to manage separate budgets
