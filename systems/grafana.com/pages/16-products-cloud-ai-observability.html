Agent Observability

Agent Observability extends AI observability to production AI agents, giving engineering teams the visibility they need to understand behavior, evaluate quality, and optimize cost and performance.

Observe and optimize production AI agents

  • Understand how production AI agents behave with visibility into conversations, tool calls, latency, cost, and quality.

  • Replay every agent decision to debug workflows and understand how responses were generated.

  • Run live evaluations to detect hallucinations, unsafe output, and quality drift before users notice.

  • Optimize latency, token usage, and costs across agents, runs, and models.
