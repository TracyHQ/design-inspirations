U.S. Open Day 9 — Latest

The fourth-round action is concluding with today’s night session at the 2026 U.S. Open.

Coco Gauff (4) has beaten Iva Jović (14) in an all-American clash on Arthur Ashe for the last women’s quarterfinal spot, with Alexander Zverev (1) currently dominating Luciano Darderi (21) in the night cap.

Learner Tien (14) lost in five sets to Karen Khachanov on Louis Armstrong while earlier, Iga Świątek (8) was stunned 7-5, 6-3 by Zheng Qinwen despite leading the first set 5-0.

Elena Rybakina (2) saw off Naomi Osaka (13) and Mirra Andreeva (5) is also into the quarters.

Darderi comes to life

Zverev 6-2, *4-1 Darderi

Well, in the previous meeting between these two at the Rome Masters in May, Luciano Darderi lost the first set 6-1 and was down a break for most of the second — before getting back to win that in a tiebreak and complete a three-set victory.

Here, the Italian gets his first hold of the second set as he tries repeatedly — and fails — to get past Alexander Zverev’s netside vigil. Eventually Darderi pulls out the lob and earns the space to win the point, before finally loosening up and finding some rhythm on his first serve to sew up the game.

Unsurprisingly, it feels like Arthur Ashe would like some competitive tennis here.
