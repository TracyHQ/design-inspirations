U.S. Open Day 8 — Recap

The round of 16 rolled along as several favorites won in the 2026 U.S. Open in Flushing Meadows, N.Y.

Carlos Alcaraz (2), Aryna Sabalenka (1), Jessica Pegula (3), Emma Navarro (26) and Ben Shelton (8) all won in straight sets, while Linda Noskova (6) needed three sets to advance. Frances Tiafoe (11) turned in a dazzling performance to upset Daniil Medvedev (7) and return to his third U.S. Open quarterfinal in four years.

A look ahead to tomorrow

The Round of 16 continues at the U.S. Open tomorrow. The highlight of the slate is an all-American bout between No. 14 Iva Jovic and No. 4 Coco Gauff, the 2023 U.S. Open champion, to kick off the night session at Arthur Ashe Stadium at 7 p.m. That will be followed by men’s No. 1 seed Alexander Zverev in action against No. 21 seed Luciano Darderi.

Other notable names playing in the Round of 16 tomorrow include Iga Świątek, Mirra Andreeva, Learner Tien and a tasty matchup between 2020 U.S. Open champion Naomi Osaka and world No. 2 Elena Rybakina.

And yes, we’ll have it all covered for you right here on The Athletic!

Let's hear from Emma Navarro

Emma Navarro, 2024 U.S. Open semifinalist and now 2026 U.S. Open quarterfinalist, on her return to the main court of the U.S. Open:

💬 “It feels good to be back on Ashe. It’s been a couple years, and I missed it a ton. I love playing out here. Thank you guys for staying late tonight. ... I definitely get my calmness from my mom. We make fun of my dad because he’s always up there flinching and moving around. I think I can hear his feet at some points when I’m hitting the ball.”

As for the emotions she dealt with during her win over Anna Kalinskaya:

💬 “I think maybe I served a little bit better and maybe she didn't serve as well as she did in the past. First set was kind of neck-and-neck, had some trouble making some headway against her serve but got it when I needed to and then felt pretty comfortable there in the second set.
