Water underpins much of human existence — contributing to good health, food production, and the energy that powers businesses. It supports an estimated 1.7 billion jobs worldwide. Yet for billions of people and entire economies, water insecurity remains a fundamental barrier to growth.

large
lp-heading-bottom-xxhuge
col-xs-12
col-sm-4
col-md-4
col-lg-4

Failing water systems limit productivity, weaken food and energy security, undermine public health, and deter private investment. Water Forward, a new multistakeholder coalition, aims to change that — moving water from a source of risk to a driver of jobs, growth, and resilience. The goal: making water systems investable, scalable, and capable of supporting prosperity at scale.

The World Bank Group is at the heart of this effort. Through its Water Strategy Implementation Plan — with an ambitious target of improving water security for 400 million people by 2030 — it brings a proven framework, global reach, and delivery capacity as its core contribution to Water Forward.

The Water Forward approach enables action—and results—at a scale no single institution could achieve on its own, laying the foundation for greater water security for as many as one billion people, and boosting job creation, productivity, and long-term prosperity.

Water Security by 2030

This commitment is anchored in the World Bank Group Water Strategy and its Implementation Plan, bringing a tested framework, global reach, and delivery capacity to help make Water Forward's goals a concrete reality.

The World Bank Group Water Department leads the institution’s efforts to advance water security as a foundation for growth, jobs, and a livable planet. The Water Department brings together financing, global knowledge, and implementation expertise to deliver transformative and scalable solutions across three pillars—Water for People, Water for Food, and Water for the Planet—to help countries build the capacity to meet global water challenges.

Water Forward Partners

large
Water Forward brings together a diverse coalition of countries, financing institutions, technical agencies, philanthropies, and private-sector leaders committed to transforming water security at scale. Each partner plays a distinct role—mobilizing finance, enabling reforms, strengthening institutions, and accelerating delivery through country-led Water Compacts. Together, these partnerships make it possible to unlock investment, improve services, and deliver water security for hundreds of millions of people.

Driving national water security ambitions
