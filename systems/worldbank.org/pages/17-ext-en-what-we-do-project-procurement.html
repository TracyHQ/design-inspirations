Explore business opportunities

The World Bank supports sustainable development with major public sector investments in education, energy and natural resources, health, transportation, and more.  Although every World Bank investment project is different, they have one thing in common: they require the procurement of goods, civil works, and/or services to deliver their development outcomes. The World Bank's Procurement Framework helps governments design project procurement strategies that ensure value for money, efficiency, transparency, and fairness while providing business opportunities to domestic and international companies.
