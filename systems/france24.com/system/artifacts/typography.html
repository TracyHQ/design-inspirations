The lead paragraph sets the pace: one idea, said once, in France 24's voice.

Body · 14px

Body text carries the argument. It should read at arm's length on a phone and stay calm on a wide screen, with a measure of about seventy characters and a line height of 1.57.

Small · 12px

Small text is for metadata, timestamps and helper lines, never for anything the reader must not miss.
