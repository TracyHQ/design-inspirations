Information for Faculty & Staff

We Are Stanford

As a Stanford faculty or staff member, you are part of a vibrant, collaborative community advancing the frontiers of knowledge and preparing a next generation of engaged citizens and leaders. The university is committed to enabling a positive and productive workplace and equipping you with the support and services you need to thrive.

Stanford AI Playground

The Stanford AI Playground is a user-friendly platform, built on open-source technologies, where you can safely try AI models from vendors like Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI in one spot. Your low and moderate-risk data data here is protected and will not be used to train AI models.

Health & Wellness

Programs to help you thrive

Perks

BeWell

The BeWell program offers a cash incentive for participating in activities, including coaching and classes, that support your well-being.

Faculty Resources

Faculty Staff Help Center

Stanford’s Faculty Staff Help Center offers professional and confidential counseling for individuals, couples, and families, to address both work and personal issues. The center also offers workshops, discussion groups, consultation, and training and facilitation services for difficult conversations. 

All services – including 10 counseling sessions per situation for employees and eligible dependents – are free of charge.

Faculty Resources

Office of the Ombuds

The University Ombuds is a safe, confidential place for faculty and staff, as well as students and postdocs, to discuss their Stanford-related concerns. The Ombuds will listen without judgment; provide information on Stanford policies and procedures; connect you to other resources; and work with you to develop a plan to address your concerns or manage conflict.

Perks

Rec & Wellness Classes

Stanford Recreation & Wellness offers fitness assessments and personal training, virtual and in-person group fitness classes, and recreational classes for all skill levels in tennis, golf, climbing, pickleball, and much more.
