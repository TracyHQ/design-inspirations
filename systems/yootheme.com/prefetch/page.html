All features you need
to build modern websites

View features

Site Builder

Build your site from top to bottom – from header and footer to dynamic templates for every page, mega menus and live search.

Page Builder

Create responsive page layouts with drag and drop. Start with a pre-built layout and add elements like sliders, grids, galleries and more.

Style Editor

Design the look of your website with colors, spacings and typography. Choose a beautiful style and refine its global UI components.

Effects & Interactions

Bring layouts to life with scroll-driven animations, sticky effects, blending content, lightbox galleries and interactive filters.

Dynamic Content

Keep your content up to date by loading posts, taxonomies and their custom fields into any builder element in your layouts.

E-Commerce

Build modern WooCommerce shops with the page builder and all its features and display products anywhere on your site.

Performance

Boost your site speed and Core Web Vitals with responsive images in next-gen formats and lazy or on click loading videos.

Accessibility

Create accessible websites with keyboard navigation, semantic HTML and ARIA that support assistive technologies.

Privacy

Protect user privacy with a consent manager for scripts, cookies and embedded content and load assets like Google Fonts locally.

System & Integrations

Work seamlessly with Joomla and WordPress features, including multilingual sites, and popular 3rd-party plugins.

Developers

Easily extend your site with custom functionalities without having to worry about their integration, distribution and versioning.
