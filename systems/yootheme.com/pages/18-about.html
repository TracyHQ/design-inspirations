About Us

At YOOtheme we're passionate about developing everything you need to create beautiful websites with WordPress and Joomla. As the market leader for Joomla themes and extensions with 20 years of experience we have one of the largest communities. We are also known as the creators of the popular Open Source project UIkit. Our small, diverse team from Hamburg, Germany, combines love for design with modern technology to make the web look good.

Our Office

We are located in the Hafencity district in the heart of our home city Hamburg. Our 700m2 office offers everything for an inspiring and motivating work atmosphere.

Our office has comfortable work and conference areas as well as a fully equipped kitchen and a large chill-out zone for some quality time with colleagues.

During the sunny days we spend our lunch barbecuing on our roof terrace with a view of the port. Or we sit down together at the kitchen table and play some games.
