User Interface

Manage Content in One Place

Widgetkit comes with an intuitive and clearly arranged user interface which allows you to easily manage your Joomla and Wordpress gallery, slider and slideshow.

Select Content Type and Widget

First, choose whether you want to create custom content or load dynamic content from Joomla or WordPress itself or a 3rd party plugin. Now select a widget which fits your needs best to display your content. Both can be changed any time later.

Add Your Content

Creating new content is easy as pie. Add a single content item or select multiple items at once through the Joomla or WordPress media library. The widget settings are just one tab away, so you can configure your Joomla or WordPress gallery, slider or slideshow on the fly.

Manage Your Widgets

All your galleries, sliders and slideshows are displayed in the widget manager. Here you can create new widgets, or edit existing ones. To insert a widget into your post, click its title, and a widget shortcode will be generated.

Powerful Gallery Features

Everything That Makes Widgetkit One of a Kind

Image Sizing

Images are automatically cropped and resized which makes publishing Joomla and WordPress galleries simple as ABC!

Fancy Animations

Widgetkit provides a bunch of eye-catching transition effects for your Joomla and WordPress slider and slideshow.

Drag & Drop Ordering

Widgetkit is intuitive and easy to use. Order gallery items via drag & drop and re-order them at any time.

Fast Loading

CSS and JavaScript files are combined, minified and cached on the fly. We also use lazy loading for efficiency.

Touch Screen Support

Widgetkit supports touch gestures for mobile devices and keyboard navigation to provide a great usability.

Theme Support

All widgets adapt their look to fit your Joomla or WordPress theme. Most Warp 7 themes even have bonus widgets.

Custom Fields

Besides the predefined fields you can add custom fields, so you can easily extend items and add more content.

1-Click Updates

When a new Widgetkit release is available, you'll be notified by Joomla or WordPress and can update with just one click.

Extendable

Widgetkit is very modular and extendable by design. You can easily add your own widgets or content providers.

Dynamic Content

Powerful Content Provider

Load dynamic content from popular 3rd party Joomla or WordPress plugins and other external web services into your widgets.
